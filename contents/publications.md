#### Selected Research

- <strong>Dongxing Zuo</strong> is leading research on curriculum ideological and political reform for new-media audiovisual program studies under a Ministry of Education industry-university collaborative education project.  
  <strong>左冬兴</strong>主持教育部产学合作协同育人项目《新媒体网络视听节目研究课程思政教学改革研究》。

- He published "Innovative Communication Strategies of Campus Media in Online Ideological and Political Education in Universities" as the first author in <strong>China Flights</strong>, 2024.  
  他以第一作者在《中国航班》2024年第14期发表《校园媒体在高校网络思政教育中的创新传播策略研究》。

- He published "Research on Career Planning Paths for College Students under the Background of Internationalized Education in Private Universities" as the first author in <strong>Da Wuhan</strong>, 2023.  
  他以第一作者在《大武汉》2023年第24期发表《民办高校国际化办学背景下大学生职业规划路径研究》。

- His graduate thesis examined debates over creativity in Chinese advertising through a case study of Ye Maozhong's advertising philosophy.  
  他的硕士论文以叶茂中广告哲学为案例，研究中国广告创意之争。
